A rail on a drone lane's track lists only that track's machines; the lane's other tracks no longer mix in
A rail card now shows what the machines attached to the rail's network can make, read from the game's own data, so it works with nothing moving and along the whole line, loaded or not: max is the makers' output with nobody pulling, min what is left with every machine using the item running full out, and now what is left with only the machines asking for it right now, all capped at what the rail carries
The From and To rows name every machine making or taking the item, however far along the line, with its rate running full out under max and right now under now
Storage and stations on a rail's network pass items along and never count as makers
A machine card shows over each ingredient how many it needs per minute at full speed, and under an ingredient the machine is short of, a red "short N/min" and a red outline. N is the need minus what has arrived in the input store over the last minute (measured while you look at the machine, so it settles after a few seconds). A machine the game reports as without electricity reads "No power" in its header and blames no ingredient. Figures no longer overlap at any ingredient count
A machine card's output per minute now uses the machine's live speed, the same as the ingredient rows, so the two always agree at any speed
Drone rails show the crafters that feed them, one row per item, as three potentials in items per minute: max (the sources' output, capped at what the rail can carry at its drone speed and spacing), min (what is left if every machine taking the item off the line ahead runs full out) and now (what is left given the machines asking for it right now)
A stretch right after a machine's input shows less than the stretch before it. Machines are found from the rail layout, so a rail whose start is not loaded shows no machines ahead of it
The rail's capacity is read on its own: where it cannot be read max shows ? and the rest of the row stays
A rail card shows six rows in all
Sources are traced back against the flow: through poles, junctions and mergers, along a lane's own track, and through storage to the crafters filling it
Each rail on a platform shows its own sources; a factory's input rails no longer show the factory they feed
A lane's tracks stay apart even before the lane has loaded. Buildings that have not loaded do not count
A rail passing through a drill's frame no longer shows the drill
Idle crafters count with the recipe they are set to

A rail card lists where its items come from and go to under its item rates, including machines, storage and stations out of render range
