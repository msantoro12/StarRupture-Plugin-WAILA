Drone rail card: aim at a drone rail and it shows, per item, the item and its totals, a "From" line (the buildings making it, one kind together with a count, the biggest first, at most three kinds then "+N more"), a "To" line (the buildings taking it), a "Stored 18.6k in 3x Storage Depot v.2" line when storage on the network holds it ("Stored 0 in ..." when the storage on the line is empty of it), and a "min A · max B · current C /min" line. Long From, To and Stored lines continue on a second line. It works with nothing moving and along the whole line, out of render range too, because it is read from the game's own drone logistics
A rail card header shows the rail's capacity next to its name ("Rail v.3  480/min") and "From N to M" (a building counts for each item it makes or takes) with a status dot: green when the machines asking for an item right now want no more than the rail can bring, orange when they want more, red when a maker feeding the rail is starved, grey when nothing takes from it. An item only storage supplies, or a Cargo Receiver not yet seen sending, is judged by its stock: green while the storage holds some or the receiver brings it, orange only when the storage is empty and a machine is asking for it; a receiver with a measured rate counts like any maker
A rail card draws a shortage in colour: when the machines asking for an item right now want more than the rail can bring, the item gets an orange "Short N/min" line after its To line and the machines on the To line are orange; when a maker feeding the rail is starved, its name on the From line is red (a kind with starved and running machines is split, "Furnace v.2" red and "2x Furnace v.2" plain)
A rail's min, max and current are all about the makers feeding it: max is what they make at full speed, min what they can make with the inputs they get now (a starved maker counts 0), both capped at what the rail carries; current is what is moving now, the smaller of what the makers put out right now and what the machines asking for it right now want. Taking machines never lower min or max, and current can sit anywhere from 0 to max
Cargo Receivers and Senders are counted at a measured rate: the items in one package over the time between two sends, summed per receiver (what it puts on the rail) and per sender (what it takes off). The send interval stands in until a connection has been seen sending twice, and a connection never seen sending reads "?" (a receiver's max and current then read the makers' part followed by "+?", or "?" for an item only receivers bring). WAILA samples the connections about once a second even when no rail is aimed at, so a rate is ready when you aim. A connection that has stopped sending counts 0 in current; a receiver whose connections have all stopped counts 0 in min too and shows red on the From line
The game reads a connection's send time as -1 between sends, so WAILA keeps the last real send time: a Cargo Receiver's or Sender's rate holds at the package size over the send interval, and a connection waiting for its next send is not counted as stopped
The items a Cargo Receiver brings and a Cargo Sender takes are the ones its connections name, so an item no building makes or asks for still shows. A Cargo Receiver is listed as a source ("From Cargo Receiver") and a Cargo Sender as a taker; a rail fed only by a receiver lists it as the source
Aiming at a drone rail looks through the drones riding it: the trace skips a drone it hits and lands on the rail behind, so the card holds steady when a drone passes the crosshair
A rail walks past storage both ways: the machines behind a depot are makers ("From 4x Assembler via Storage Depot v.2"), the machines taking from it are takers, and chains of depots pass through. An item only a depot holds reads "From Storage Depot v.2" with its stock and totals of 0
Makers are traced back against the flow: through poles, junctions and mergers, along a lane's own track (a lane's other tracks stay apart, even before the lane has loaded), and through storage to the crafters filling it. Buildings that have not loaded do not count, and a rail passing through a drill's frame does not show the drill
A rail's takers are the machines after its end only; a machine taking off the line before the rail does not count against it. When the walk finds no maker or taker for an item, the game's own delivery requests supply the source or destination, so the item still shows. A crafter at an end shows what it takes for an ingredient the rail carries, as well as what it makes. Idle crafters count with the recipe they are set to
A rail with no building linked reads "Nothing connected", and "Rail data not available" when the rail's network cannot be read on this game build. The rail's capacity is read on its own: where it cannot be read max shows ? and the rest of the row stays
Buildings out of render range are named from the game's Mass data instead of reading "Out of range"; only a building the data cannot name reads "Unknown building"
The card follows the camera, so it works from the building drone; Max Distance counts from the drone
A machine card shows over each ingredient how many it needs per minute at the machine's speed, and under an ingredient the machine is short of, a red "short N/min" and a red outline. N is the need minus what has arrived in the input store over the last minute (measured while you look at the machine, so it needs about 20 seconds of looking before it shows). A machine the game reports as without electricity reads "No power" in its header and blames no ingredient. Figures do not overlap at any ingredient count
A machine card's output per minute uses the machine's live speed, the same as the ingredient rows, so the two always agree at any speed
